About Emacs Themes Site

A curated directory of the best themes for the world's most extensible editor.

This tries to be a comprehensive resource for those interested in Emacs themes. It is a collection of popular and underground, old and new Emacs Themes.

If you want to add you own theme go to GitHub. But if you find it hard to do it just email me.

Tip!

I don’t have the time and energy to list all the themes but if you want your theme to be listed or have any suggestions/thoughts regarding the site, email me!
